Road Accident Analysis & Prevention

This project analyzes road accident data for different States/UTs and different years. The analysis focuses on accidents, persons killed, persons injured and fatal accidents.

The objective of this project is to identify accident trends, high-risk states and important areas where road accident prevention measures can be applied.

🛡 Road Accident Prevention

Based on the accident, killed, injured and fatal accident analysis, the following preventive measures can help reduce road accidents and their severity.

🚦 1. Follow Traffic Rules

Drivers should follow traffic signals, speed limits, lane rules and other road safety regulations.

🪖 2. Always Wear a Helmet

Two-wheeler riders and passengers should always use properly fitted helmets to reduce the severity of head injuries.

🚗 3. Avoid Overspeeding

Speed should be controlled according to road conditions, traffic and weather conditions.

📵 4. Avoid Mobile Phone Use

Drivers should not use mobile phones while driving because distraction can increase accident risk.

🍺 5. No Drunk Driving

Driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs should be strictly avoided.

🌧 6. Drive Carefully in Bad Weather

During rain, fog or poor visibility, drivers should reduce speed and maintain a safe distance.

🛣 7. Improve High-Risk Roads

High-risk states and accident-prone locations should receive better road design, lighting, signs and maintenance.

🚑 8. Improve Emergency Response

Fast ambulance services and emergency medical facilities can help reduce deaths after serious accidents.

📊 9. Use Data for Prevention

Accident trends and state-wise risk analysis should be continuously monitored to identify areas requiring additional road safety measures.
